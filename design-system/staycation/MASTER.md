# Design System Master File — Monte Azur

> **REFERENCE ALIGNMENT:** Modeled after the architectural studio visual identity:
> Warm limestone/oatmeal canvas, high-contrast editorial typography, asymmetric 3-column spaces mosaic, and floating action pill.

---

**Project:** Monte Azur (Staycation & Private Villa)  
**Aesthetic:** Architectural Brutalist Luxury & Minimalist Editorial  
**Color Profile:** Limestone Sand & Deep Architectural Charcoal  

---

### Typography

- **Display Headline Font:** Cormorant Garamond / Playfair Display (Serif, weights 300, 400, 500)
- **Body & Label Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-serif, weights 400, 500, 600, 700)
- **Tracking:** Headings tight (`-0.015em`), uppercase labels wide (`0.18em` to `0.25em`)

### Key Components

1. **Editorial Hero:** Full-bleed photograph with centered "Design that lasts" and horizontal rule subline.
2. **Spaces Mosaic Grid:** 3-column asymmetric layout (Column 1: Intro + Courtyard card; Column 2: Tall Hillside card + landscape card; Column 3: 3 stacked horizontal cards).
3. **Honors Ribbon:** Clean 5-column architectural recognition bar.
4. **Floating Action Pill:** Sleek dark pill on bottom-left for immediate booking access.
5. **Interactive Calendar Engine:** Leap year calculation, range picker, and transparent itemized quote.
