THE RESIDENCE —

More than a place to stay. A space to reconnect.

Perched high on the sunlit cliffs above the bay, Monte Azur was conceived as a private architectural retreat where the boundaries between interior solace and the ocean horizon dissolve effortlessly.

Crafted with warm teak paneling, honed travertine terraces, and floor-to-ceiling glass expanses, every room welcomes natural daylight, ocean breezes, and panoramic dusk reflections.

Whether you are sharing a quiet breakfast overlooking the water, gathering for an evening dinner prepared in the gourmet kitchen, or taking a midnight swim beneath the stars, every space invites presence and ease.

4,800 sq ft Living & Terrace Space (446 m²)
4.5 Designer Bathrooms
2 Private Pools (Main + Plunge)
24/7 Dedicated Villa Concierge
